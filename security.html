Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack began spreading across computers worldwide. The malware encrypted files and demanded payment from victims to restore access. This attack disrupted hospitals, businesses, and government organizations, showing how dangerous outdated software and cybersecurity weaknesses can be.

Types of Malware

  2. Trojan Horses

Prevention Strategies

Cybersecurity and Cyberattacks

Cybersecurity helps protect computers, networks, and personal information from hackers and cyberattacks.

How Network Packets Work

Network packets are small pieces of data that travel between computers over a network. They help devices communicate and exchange information through the internet.

What Is Malware?

Malware is harmful software designed to damage computers, steal information, or disrupt computer systems. Common types of malware include viruses, ransomware, and spyware.

What Are Hackers?

Hackers are individuals who use technical skills to access or investigate computer systems. Some hackers help organizations identify security weaknesses, while others gain unauthorized access to steal data or cause damage.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Phishing: Fake messages designed to trick people into sharing information.

Ransomware: Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

Data Breaches: Incidents where sensitive information is accessed or disclosed without authorization.

How Can We Stay Safe Online?

People can improve cybersecurity by using strong passwords, enabling multifactor authentication, updating software, and avoiding suspicious links.
